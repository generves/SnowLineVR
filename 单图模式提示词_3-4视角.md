# 单图模式提示词（3/4 视角）
## Tripo 单图生成 3D —— 逐条可直接复制

> **适用场景**：Tripo 的**单图模式**（Image to Model），每次只上传 1 张图。
>
> **和四视图方案的区别**：
> | | 四视图方案 | 本方案（单图） |
> |---|---|---|
> | 上传图数 | 4 张（前/左/右/后） | **1 张** |
> | 视角 | 正交投影 | **3/4 透视（俯视 30° + 侧转 45°）** |
> | 精度 | 高 | 中 |
> | 成本 | 生成 4 张图 | 生成 1 张图 |
>
> ⚠️ **关键**：单图模式**必须用 3/4 视角**。正对镜头的正交正面图没有深度线索，AI 只能"猜"厚度，结果往往是**物件被做成纸片**。
>
> **尺寸不标注在图上**，尺寸已写在描述文字里；绝对尺寸在 Unity 里手动校正。

---

## 通用 Prompt 结构（每条都已写死，直接复制）

每条提示词包含三部分：
1. **固定要求** —— 3/4 视角、纯白背景、无标尺无手无文字
2. **视角定义** —— 明确相机相对物体的位置
3. **物体描述** —— 尺寸、材质、细节特征

## 道具 1｜急救夹板

```
Generate ONE single image: a THREE-QUARTER PERSPECTIVE VIEW of the object described below.

CAMERA ANGLE (critical for 3D reconstruction):
- Position the camera at roughly 45° to the object's horizontal axis and about 30° above it, looking down at the object
- This must show THREE faces of the object simultaneously: the TOP face, the FRONT face, and the RIGHT SIDE face
- Slight perspective is intentional and required (this is NOT an orthographic front view)
- The object should be rotated so its main features are clearly readable

STYLE: clean realistic product-render style, semi-realistic (not cartoonish, not hyper-detailed). Matte surfaces with subtle realistic material texture. Even diffuse studio lighting from the upper front-left. Pure white background (#FFFFFF), no shadow cast on the background, no gradients.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (critical):
- The object must be centered and occupy about 70% of the frame
- The image must contain ONLY the target object — nothing else in frame. No ruler, no scale bar, no measuring reference, no hands, no props, no supporting surface, no decorative elements. Pure white background only.
- The object must NOT be resting on any visible surface — show it floating against the pure white background, or with only an extremely subtle contact shadow directly beneath it
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no arrows
- Do NOT draw any size annotations or measurement markings anywhere in the image
- No hands, nobody holding the object
- Sharp focus throughout, high detail, resolution at least 1024x1024

OBJECT TO GENERATE:
A reusable emergency splint (moldable splint), adult forearm/leg size. Rectangular flat strip, 45 cm long × 11 cm wide × 1 cm thick. Material: matte off-white foam/plastic composite. Surface: subtle longitudinal anti-slip grooves running along the length; the strip has a gentle curve across its width so it can wrap around a limb. Edges fully rounded, not sharp. Two short velcro straps attached at roughly 1/3 and 2/3 of its length, lying flat against the surface. Color: off-white / light beige.

FOR THIS 3/4 VIEW: make sure the thinness of the slab, the gentle cross-section curve, AND the two velcro straps are all visible at once.
```

# 附：两套提示词怎么选

| | 单图模式（本文件） | 多视图模式（`四视图提示词_可直接复制.md`） |
|---|---|---|
| 生成图数 | 1 张 | 4 张 |
| 精度 | 中等 | **高** |
| 厚度还原 | 依赖透视线索，容易偏 | **准**（多视角互相校正） |
| 背面还原 | **AI 靠猜** | **有真实后视图** |
| 适合 | 形状简单的物件（夹板、手机、三角牌） | **结构复杂、有背面细节的物件**（保温毯、绷带卷、伤者） |
| 建议 | 时间紧时用 | **主力方案** |

**我的建议**：**先用两个道具各试一遍**（比如夹板用单图、绷带用四视图），**对比 Tripo 出来的质量再决定主用哪个**。
